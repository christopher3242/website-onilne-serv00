Chess rules

Capture the opposing king by checkmating it. Each player moves one piece per turn: pawns move forward and capture diagonally; knights move in an L; bishops move diagonally; rooks move horizontally or vertically; queens combine rook and bishop movement; kings move one square.

Castling, en passant, and automatic queen promotion are supported. A game can end by checkmate, resignation, stalemate, or another draw under standard chess rules.
